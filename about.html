Morning · 09:00 — how we got here

Built by people tired of busy work

Stackly started as an internal tool for a five-person studio that missed too many deadlines. Six years later, it runs the day for teams across 60 countries.

2019

A spreadsheet outgrows itself

Our founders build a shared task sheet to stop losing track of client work.

2020

First 100 teams

Stackly opens to the public as a lightweight daily planner, nothing more.

2021

Calendar sync arrives

Two-way sync with Google and Outlook removes the last reason to double-book.

2023

Stackly for Teams

Shared workspaces, permissions and reporting for growing operations teams.

2025

42,000 teams and counting

Offices in three cities, a fully remote team, and a product built in the open.
